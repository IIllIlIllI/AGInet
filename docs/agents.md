# AGInet Agent Council

AGInet experiments with a multi-agent reasoning council.

Each agent represents a different analytical perspective.

---

## Batman

Investigative reasoning agent.

Focus:
- structural analysis
- claim verification
- uncovering hidden relationships

Batman attempts to teach users how to reason through complex information.

---

## Harley

Devil’s advocate.

Focus:
- fringe hypotheses
- psychological dynamics
- extreme interpretations

Harley tests whether alternative interpretations reveal weaknesses in dominant narratives.

---

## Socrates

Logical interrogator.

Focus:
- questioning assumptions
- exposing contradictions
- clarifying definitions

---

## Athena

Strategic synthesis.

Focus:
- integrating perspectives
- identifying wisdom across viewpoints
- producing coherent conclusions

---

## Rorschach

Bias detection agent.

Focus:
- narrative framing
- ideological distortions
- psychological projection

---

## Crab

Civilizational stabilizer.

Focus:
- singularity risks
- great filter theory
- apocalypse modeling
- long-horizon technological risk

Crab studies science fiction and scientific literature about existential risks.
